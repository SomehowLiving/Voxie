Not connected. Use headphones for the most realistic test.
  • Listen first: stay silent after connecting, and Voxie greets you after ~4s. Or say "Hello?" straight away, and it answers you instead.
  • Any language: answer in Tamil, Spanish, Korean… The listener switches on your first real sentence; the "language on record" is only a starting guess.
  • Barge in: start talking while Voxie is speaking. It drops its volume at once and stops if you're really interrupting.
  • Backchannel: say "yeah, okay" while it talks. It should dip, then carry on.
  • Say "bye" to end the call.
