Hear every voice Voxie speaks with.

Every voice reads the same two lines in its language. Voxie's own voices run on your hardware; the others are providers you plug in with a key.

Each clip is the voice as it came from its provider, levelled to the same loudness. Voxie picks the voice per language from your config, and moves to another when one fails. Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Deepgram and others plug in the same way, with your own key.
